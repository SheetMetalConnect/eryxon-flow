Until now the single-workshop rule lived only in the licence text: the code was the
same multi-tenant application everywhere. 0.10.1 makes the installation behave the
way the licence reads. Self-host it for your shop and you get one workshop, unlimited,
with everyone joining by invitation. The hosted service is unchanged.
