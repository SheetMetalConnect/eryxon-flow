v0.9 is the licensing release. Eryxon Flow is source-available under the Business
Source License 1.1: read it, change it, run the Community edition for your own
workshop at no charge. Multi-site deployments, hosting it for others and the
commercial add-ons are Premium.

The same split moved native device features and the ERP planning connectors out of
Community. The Community app is a plain web application that runs on the tablets,
kiosks and phones already on the shop floor.
