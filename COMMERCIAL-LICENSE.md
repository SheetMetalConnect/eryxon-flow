# Commercial licensing — Eryxon Flow

Eryxon Flow is source-available under the [Business Source License 1.1](LICENSE).
The **Community** edition is free to self-host for a **single production site**.
This page explains when you need a commercial (**Premium**) license instead.

## When you need a commercial license

You need a Premium license if you want to:

- Run Eryxon Flow across **more than one site**;
- Offer Eryxon Flow to third parties as a **hosted or managed service**; or
- Use any **Premium** capability listed below.

Self-hosting the Community edition for one workshop, reading and modifying the
source, and any non-production use are always free under the BSL — no license
needed.

## What Premium includes

- All ERP connectors and add-ons
- Monitoring and operational tooling
- Multi-site management and the central hub dashboard
- Advanced workflows and whitelabeling
- Advanced 3D (nesting, unfolding, annotations)
- An SLA, delivered hosted (Cloud) or on your own infrastructure (on-prem)
